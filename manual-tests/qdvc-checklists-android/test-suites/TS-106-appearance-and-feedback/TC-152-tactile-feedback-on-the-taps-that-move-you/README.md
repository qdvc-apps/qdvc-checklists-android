## Tactile feedback on the taps that move you somewhere

Taps that change what is on screen are confirmed by a brief vibration.

#### Preconditions

- The device's touch-feedback or vibration setting is switched on
- A workspace with at least two checklists is added, and at least two checklists have been opened
