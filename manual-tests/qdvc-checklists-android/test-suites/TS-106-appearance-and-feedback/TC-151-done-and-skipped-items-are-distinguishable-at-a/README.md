## Done and skipped items are distinguishable at a glance

Done and skipped both mean settled, and both grey the row out, so the icon is the
only thing separating them. It has to do that job at arm's length, in every theme.

#### Preconditions

- A checklist is open with at least one item done, one skipped, and one untouched
